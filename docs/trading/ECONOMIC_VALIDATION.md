# Economic validation

`ECONOMIC_EDGE_VALIDATED = NO`.

The system has causal research infrastructure, explicit costs and immutable artifacts. Passing arithmetic/state-machine tests and synthetic backtests do not prove edge. There is no sealed multi-asset real-data study with provider provenance, point-in-time universes, walk-forward folds, untouched final holdout, fees/slippage/liquidity, regimes, tail events, drawdown/ruin analysis and sustained paper/shadow evidence.

Future work must pre-register the protocol, version data/features/models, isolate the final holdout, include delisted/survivorship effects where relevant and report uncertainty and failures. Final-test optimization is prohibited and profitability cannot be guaranteed.
