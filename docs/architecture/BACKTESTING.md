# Offline causal backtesting

Phase 21 provides an event-driven research simulator in the Python modular monolith.
It accepts one immutable, quality-validated crypto spot dataset and supports LONG inventory
and sales of owned inventory only. SHORT, leverage, derivatives, equities, ETFs, borrow,
funding and corporate actions are unsupported. Single-market selection and survivorship
bias remain unassessed; results cannot establish universe performance or profitability.

## Availability and boundaries

The explicit RECORDED_RECEIPT policy preserves each candle received_at in UTC. The Phase 20
default dataset replay remains unchanged. Contiguous known candle prefixes feed the shared
versioned technical intelligence kernel. Strategy callbacks receive immutable bounded views
and only matching forecasts generated by the cutoff and still available before expiry.
Full immutable forecast artifacts are persisted separately from their outcomes.
Callbacks are trusted research code, not a security sandbox; they cannot access a broker
through this interface. This phase adds no strategy promotion or production execution path.

Every order requires versioned risk approval before acknowledgement and simulated execution.
Cash and inventory reservations prevent concurrent overspending and naked sales. Kill switch,
stale data, spread, exposure, worst-case debit, pending-order, daily-loss and drawdown checks
fail closed. A risk halt cancels pending orders and prevents new ones; it does not fabricate
liquidation fills or guarantee drawdown limits on existing inventory.

## Execution and accounting

A fill requires a completed candle whose open is strictly later than the order ready time.
Its recorded receipt is the observation time. There are no same-bar or retrospective fills,
no best-extreme fills, and no forced liquidation at dataset end. Latency, expiry, cancellation,
idempotent intent IDs, partial fills and rejections are audited chronologically. FIFO orders
share one participation-limited bar capacity. Cumulative impact prevents order splitting
from resetting liquidity impact. The future complete-bar CLOSE is an explicit price proxy,
not an observed midquote or actual exchange execution.

Fees and spread must be positive; adverse slippage and impact are explicit configurable
assumptions. Costs are incorporated once in cash and inventory cost basis, and decomposed
for reporting. Decimal precision 80 and UTC govern accounting. Quantity steps and minimums
are checked, but continuous-price arithmetic does not reproduce exchange price ticks,
quote rounding, minimum fee schedules or measured intrabar liquidity. Cost parameters
must be independently validated for serious economic research.

## Reproducibility and persistence

RunConfig requires dataset identity/hash, strategy/model/feature/cost/risk versions,
parameters, declared git revision, verified source-tree and environment hashes, seed,
selection rationale and evaluation split. The engine uses no random generator. External
callback determinism is the caller's responsibility; differing persisted results for the
same manifest are rejected. Public FINAL_HOLDOUT runs are blocked; Phase 22 supplies protected one-shot research
factory authorization with durable consumption and frozen fitted artifacts. No final-test optimization facility is provided.

BacktestService loads the frozen dataset, runs Backtester and stores a validated report
using BacktestRepository; the caller commits the SQLAlchemy transaction. Reports include
orders, fills, equity, closed inventory cohorts, metrics, full forecast inputs and hashes.
Reload verifies payload, indexes, parent dataset provenance and accounting reconciliation.
Content hashes detect corruption, not malicious authenticated replacement. Identical reruns
return the first immutable report; conflicting results fail. GET /api/v1/backtests/{run_id}
is read-only, no-store, and returns generic dependency/corruption errors. There is no POST.
Migration 0012 adds backtest_reports and indexes with a dataset foreign key. Downgrade
removes report data only; export reports before rollback.

## Metrics and limits

Metrics cover net return, CAGR, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, maximum drawdown and
its duration, closed-cohort profit factor, expectancy and win/loss counts, realized PnL,
turnover, exposure/time in market, cost decomposition, bankruptcy episodes, drawdown
breaches and empirical tail loss. Partial exits are not independent completed trades.
CAGR requires at least one configured year. Annualized ratios require regular receipt
intervals and sufficient returns; zero denominators yield unavailable values with reasons.
Empirical tail estimates require adequate sample size and regular intervals. These are
descriptive observations, not calibrated confidence or forecasts. Ruin probability is
unavailable without a validated model. Close marks cannot estimate intrabar drawdowns.
Synthetic fixtures only test mechanics and never establish profitability. Live readiness
and profitability claims are always false. Live execution remains disabled by default.
