# Pocket Alpha
Pocket Alpha is multi-asset quantitative investment intelligence and, after independent validation,
autonomous trading. Its objective is long-term NET compounded equity growth after fees, spread,
slippage, execution costs and realized losses, constrained by drawdown, liquidity and risk of ruin.
Accuracy, win rate and gross backtest return do not establish economic value.

One intelligence layer serves manual investors, active traders, portfolio investors and strategies.
Initial assets: crypto, stocks, ETFs and indices; extensible to forex, commodities, bonds, options
and futures. Search, charting, analysis, watchlists, opportunity scanning, portfolio intelligence,
research, paper trading and eventually autopilot are successive experiences over the same domain.
Forecasts cover 1H through 12M independently of candle resolution. LONG and SHORT analysis remain
independent of broker capabilities. NO_TRADE is a legitimate result.

All displayed analysis must derive from stored legitimate data and calculations. Missing data means
unavailable, never plausible-looking estimates. Pocket Score describes setup quality; Opportunity
Score describes a specific horizon/trade after costs; neither is calibrated probability.
Every recommendation exposes evidence, opposition, uncertainty and version.
Autopilot ultimately handles allocation, risk, exits and compounding with configurable cash reserves
and profit protection. It cannot bypass research validation or production authorization.
